RESEARCH

Wireless Communications Research

Mathematical modeling and performance analysis of wireless receiver techniques.

MENG PROJECT· UNIVERSITY OF VICTORIA · 2007

Analysis of Switch Diversity with Post-Exam Selection in Generalized Correlated Fading Environment

Author: Araya Chaowalit
Supervisor: Dr. Hong-Chuan Yang

Investigated how a wireless receiver can select among fading signal paths while balancing communication reliability and receiver complexity.

  • Analyzed switch-and-examine combining with post-examining selection (SECps) under correlated Nakagami-m fading.
  • Derived output signal-to-noise ratio statistics and evaluated outage probability and average error rate.
  • Examined tradeoffs involving switching thresholds, path estimation requirements and switching probability.
  • Demonstrated that channel correlation can materially degrade performance and should be considered in performance predictions.
Wireless Communications Statistical Modeling Performance Analysis Engineering Tradeoffs
