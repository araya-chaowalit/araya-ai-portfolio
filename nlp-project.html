NLP MODEL FINE-TUNING

Detecting rare policy violations in multilingual messages.

The project fine-tunes multilingual DistilBERT for a highly imbalanced binary-classification problem involving Thai and English text.

It demonstrates data validation, Transformer fine-tuning, validation-based threshold optimization, held-out testing, error analysis and responsible human oversight.

Positive-class performance

99.32%Accuracy
85.71%Precision
60.00%Recall

Result interpretation

The model detected 6 of 10 positive test examples and generated one false-positive alert. Precision, recall and F1 are more informative than accuracy for this highly imbalanced dataset.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Privacy, limitations and human review

Data privacy
Original messages and user identities are not published; public samples are synthetic.
Limited positives
Only 44 positive examples are labeled, including 10 in the test set, so results are preliminary.
Human oversight
Predictions prioritize review and should not independently determine punitive actions.
